About Wei-Yi Lai

Wei-Yi Lai brings a cross-disciplinary background in music performance, education, and brand management. For over three decades, she has been dedicated to harp and piano instruction in Taichung, Taiwan, integrating performance, conducting, and educational psychology.

She specializes in music-assisted therapy for seniors, combining music therapy with psychology to empower elderly individuals through artistic expression. She is also the founder of Hope Harp, a harp manufacturing brand with custom designs and global OEM production, holding two Taiwan patents (No. I903394 & I891530).

Currently serving as an adjunct lecturer at Chinese Culture University (Taichung Extension), she teaches courses in senior art therapy, senior music therapy, and small harp ensemble instructor training, advancing music education and art therapy in Taiwan.

What is Hope Harp and is it a real brand?

Hope Harp (希望豎琴) is a lever harp brand founded by Wei-Yi Lai under AHS Music Pty. Ltd., an Australian company; its harps are designed in Australia and made in Taiwan. Who is senior music and art therapy suitable for? What are the benefits?

Senior music and art therapy is ideal for adults aged 55+, long-term care facility staff, and family caregivers. Combining music, visual arts, and movement, it offers seniors a relaxing, engaging activity with more social interaction. Wei-Yi Lai's 12-week structured program has been implemented in multiple care facilities in Taichung and is offered as a certified instructor training course at Chinese Culture University.

Is harp difficult to learn? How long before I can play a song?

Harp is surprisingly approachable! Most beginners can play their first complete piece within 4–8 weeks. Wei-Yi Lai tailors the pace to each student, making it effective even for those with no prior music experience. The harp's beautiful tone is gentle on fingers and suitable for children, adults, and seniors alike—making it one of the most rewarding instruments to pick up.

What is the difference between lever harp and pedal harp?

A lever harp (like Hope Harp) uses small levers on each string to change pitch by a semitone, making it lightweight, portable, and ideal for beginners, folk music, and therapy settings. A pedal harp uses foot pedals for chromatic changes and is used in orchestras. Hope Harp's 27 and 36 models are lever harps suitable for ABRSM Grade 1–8 exams and most popular music repertoire.

About Wei-Yi Lai

Wei-Yi Lai holds a Master of Music from The Ohio State University, bringing a cross-disciplinary background in music performance, education, and brand management. For over three decades, she has been dedicated to harp and piano instruction in Taichung, Taiwan, integrating performance, conducting, and educational psychology.

She specializes in music-assisted therapy for seniors, combining music therapy with psychology to empower elderly individuals through artistic expression. She is also the founder of HopeHarp, a harp manufacturing brand with custom designs and global OEM production, holding two Taiwan patents (No. I903394 & I891530).

Currently serving as an adjunct lecturer at Chinese Culture University (Taichung Extension), she teaches courses in senior art therapy, senior music therapy, and small harp ensemble instructor training, advancing music education and art therapy in Taiwan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Wei-Yi Lai and what does she teach?

Wei-Yi Lai (賴薇伊) is a Taiwan-based harp educator with a Master of Music from The Ohio State University and over 30 years of teaching experience. She offers private and group harp lessons (beginner to ABRSM Grade 1–8), piano and keyboard instruction, senior music and art therapy programs, and harp ensemble instructor training. Lessons are available in-person in Taichung or online.

What is HopeHarp and is it a real brand?

HopeHarp (希望豎琴) is a lever harp brand founded by Wei-Yi Lai under AHS Music Pty. Ltd., originating from Australia. It holds two Taiwan invention patents: I903394 and I891530. The brand offers a complete product line from 8-string mini harps (35 cm, 0.6 kg) to 40-string concert harps (160 cm, 23 kg), and is the only lever harp brand in Taiwan with registered invention patents.

What is the Angel Launch 2026 harp teacher training?

Angel Launch (天使啟航) 2026 is a 24-hour harp teacher incubation program designed by Wei-Yi Lai for music teachers and music store owners across Taiwan. Using HopeHarp 27S and 36S models, the program is held at Hengdao Hall in Taichung. Fee details are available by inquiry. Graduates receive a certificate and are qualified to independently lead harp ensemble classes.

Who is senior music and art therapy suitable for? What are the benefits?

Senior music and art therapy is ideal for adults aged 55+, long-term care facility staff, and family caregivers. Combining music, visual arts, and movement, it helps reduce stress, activate cognitive function, and improve social interaction. Wei-Yi Lai's 12-week structured program has been implemented in multiple care facilities in Taichung and is offered as a certified instructor training course at Chinese Culture University.

Is harp difficult to learn? How long before I can play a song?

Harp is surprisingly approachable! Most beginners can play their first complete piece within 4–8 weeks. Wei-Yi Lai tailors the pace to each student, making it effective even for those with no prior music experience. The harp's beautiful tone is gentle on fingers and suitable for children, adults, and seniors alike—making it one of the most rewarding instruments to pick up.

What is the difference between lever harp and pedal harp?

A lever harp (like HopeHarp) uses small levers on each string to change pitch by a semitone, making it lightweight, portable, and ideal for beginners, folk music, and therapy settings. A pedal harp uses foot pedals for chromatic changes and is used in orchestras. HopeHarp's 27S and 36S models are lever harps suitable for ABRSM Grade 1–8 exams and most popular music repertoire.
